## 原文

> [!note]- 英文原文（点击展开）
> <span class="highlight-pink">Resilience</span> is the ability to <span class="highlight-pink">bounce back</span> from difficult experiences. It is not a trait that people either have or do not have. It involves behaviors, thoughts, and actions that can be learned and developed by anyone.
>
> Research has shown that resilience is ordinary, not extraordinary. People commonly demonstrate resilience. One example is the response of many Americans to the September 11, 2001 terrorist attacks and individuals' efforts to rebuild their lives.
>
> <span class="highlight-orange">Like building a muscle, increasing your resilience takes time and intentionality.</span> Focusing on four core components — connection, wellness, healthy thinking, and meaning — can empower you to withstand and learn from difficult experiences.
>
> **Connection**: Prioritize relationships. Connecting with empathetic and understanding people can remind you that you're not alone in the midst of difficulties.
>
> **Wellness**: Take care of your body. Self-care may be a popular buzzword, but it's also a legitimate practice for mental health and <span class="highlight-pink">resilient</span> living.
>
> **Healthy Thinking**: Avoid seeing crises as insurmountable problems. You can't change the fact that highly stressful events happen, but you can change how you interpret and respond to these events.
>
> **Meaning**: Look for opportunities for self-discovery. Many people who have experienced tragedies and hardship have reported better relationships, greater sense of strength, and a deeper appreciation for life.

## 长难句分析

> [!example]- 长难句 1
> **原句**：Like building a muscle, increasing your resilience takes time and intentionality.
> **结构分析**：Like building a muscle（方式状语）+ increasing your resilience（动名词作主语）+ takes（谓语）+ time and intentionality（宾语）
> **翻译**：就像锻炼肌肉一样，增强韧性需要时间和有意为之。

> [!example]- 长难句 2
> **原句**：You can't change the fact that highly stressful events happen, but you can change how you interpret and respond to these events.
> **结构分析**：并列复合句。前半句 the fact 后接 that 同位语从句；后半句 how 引导宾语从句
> **翻译**：你无法改变高压力事件发生这一事实，但你可以改变你解读和应对这些事件的方式。

3. **Healthy Thinking** — 改变认知方式
4. **Meaning** — 在困难中寻找意义
