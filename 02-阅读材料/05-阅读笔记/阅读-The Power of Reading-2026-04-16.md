## 原文

> [!quote] 原文内容
> Reading is one of the most powerful tools for personal growth and development. When we read, we expose ourselves to new ideas, perspectives, and ways of thinking that we might never encounter in our daily lives.
>
> Research has consistently shown that regular reading improves vocabulary, enhances critical thinking skills, and increases empathy. A study conducted by the University of Sussex found that reading for just six minutes can reduce stress levels by up to 68 percent, making it more effective than listening to music or going for a walk.
>
> Moreover, reading helps us develop a deeper understanding of the world around us. Through literature, we can experience different cultures, historical periods, and social contexts without leaving our homes. This exposure fosters tolerance and broadens our worldview.
>
> In the digital age, where information is ubiquitous and attention spans are shrinking, the habit of deep reading has become more important than ever. While skimming and scanning have their place, sustained reading of complex texts remains essential for developing analytical skills and intellectual resilience.

## 难句解析

### 句子 1

- **原文**：Research has consistently shown that regular reading improves vocabulary, enhances critical thinking skills, and increases empathy.
- **翻译**：研究一直表明，定期阅读能改善词汇量，提升批判性思维能力，并增强同理心。
- **语法分析**：主句 Research has shown + that宾语从句，从句中三个并列动词 improves, enhances, increases
- **重点词汇**：consistently, enhance, critical thinking

### 句子 2

- **原文**：In the digital age, where information is ubiquitous and attention spans are shrinking, the habit of deep reading has become more important than ever.
- **翻译**：在信息无处不在、注意力持续时间正在缩短的数字时代，深度阅读的习惯变得比以往任何时候都更加重要。
- **语法分析**：where引导非限制性定语从句修饰 digital age，主句 the habit...has become more important than ever
- **重点词汇**：ubiquitous, shrinking, sustained

## 核心概念与观点

1. 阅读是个人成长的有力工具
2. 阅读能减压、提升词汇和批判性思维
3. 阅读拓展世界观、培养包容性
4. 数字时代深度阅读更加重要
