Reading is not merely a passive act of consuming words on a page. It is an ==active engagement== with ideas, a dialogue between the reader and the author that transcends time and space. When we read deeply, we enter a state of **flow** where the world around us fades away, and we become immersed in the narrative.

The art of reading requires discipline and patience. In our age of ==ephemeral== digital content, the ability to sit with a long-form text and extract its meaning is becoming increasingly rare. Social media has trained us to ==skim== rather than to dive deep, to seek quick dopamine hits rather than ==sustained== understanding.

Yet the rewards of deep reading are ==profound==. Research shows that reading literary fiction enhances empathy and emotional intelligence. It exposes us to diverse perspectives and challenges our preconceptions. Each book we read becomes a lens through which we can view the world differently.

To cultivate the art of reading, one must first **slow down**. Resist the urge to rush through paragraphs. Instead, pause to reflect on what you've read. Ask questions: What is the author really saying? How does this relate to what I already know? What emotions does this ==evoke==?

Second, read widely. Don't confine yourself to a single genre or subject. The beauty of reading lies in its ability to ==surprise== us, to introduce us to worlds we never knew existed. A physicist might find inspiration in poetry; a historian might discover insights in a novel.

Finally, read with a pen in hand. ==Annotate==, underline, and write marginal notes. The act of marking up a text forces you to engage with it more deeply. It transforms reading from a passive activity into an active conversation.

## 文章摘要

### 英文摘要

Deep reading is an active engagement that requires discipline. In the digital age, we tend to skim rather than read deeply, but the rewards of deep reading are profound. To cultivate this art: slow down, read widely, and annotate actively.

### 中文摘要

深度阅读是一种需要自律的积极参与。在数字时代，我们倾向于略读而非深读，但深读的回报是深刻的。培养阅读艺术的方法：放慢速度、广泛阅读、积极批注。
