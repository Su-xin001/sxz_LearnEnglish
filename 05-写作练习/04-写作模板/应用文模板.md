# 📋 应用文写作模板

## 1. 书信模板

### 正式书信

> Dear **[称谓]**,
>
> I am writing to **[写信目的]**.
>
> **[正文第一段：说明情况]**
>
> **[正文第二段：具体请求/建议]**
>
> **[正文第三段：期望/感谢]**
>
> I would appreciate it if you could **[请求]**. Thank you for your time and consideration.
>
> Yours sincerely,
> **[姓名]**

### 非正式书信

> Dear **[名字]**,
>
> How have you been? I hope everything is going well with you.
>
> **[正文]**
>
> Looking forward to hearing from you soon.
>
> Best wishes,
> **[姓名]**

## 2. 电子邮件模板

> Subject: **[主题]**
>
> Dear **[收件人]**,
>
> I am writing regarding **[事由]**.
>
> **[正文]**
>
> Please let me know if you have any questions.
>
> Best regards,
> **[姓名]**

## 3. 通知模板

> **NOTICE**
>
> **[通知主题]**
>
> This is to inform you that **[通知内容]**.
>
> - Date: **[日期]**
> - Time: **[时间]**
> - Venue: **[地点]**
> - Requirements: **[要求]**
>
> All concerned are requested to **[行动要求]**.
>
> **[发布单位]**
> **[日期]**

## 4. 简历模板

## 常用书信表达

| 功能 | 正式表达 | 非正式表达 |
|------|----------|------------|
| 开头 | I am writing to... | Just wanted to... |
| 请求 | I would be grateful if... | Could you... |
| 道歉 | I sincerely apologize for... | Sorry about... |
| 感谢 | I appreciate your... | Thanks for... |
| 结尾 | I look forward to your reply. | Write back soon! |
