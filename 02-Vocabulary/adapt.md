# adapt

## Definition

**v.** to make suitable to requirements or conditions; to adjust

## Examples

- We must **adapt** to the changing environment.
- The novel was **adapted** into a film.
- She quickly **adapted** to her new school.

## Collocations

- adapt to 适应
- adapt oneself 使自己适应
- adapt for 为...改编
- adapt from 根据...改编

## Related Words

- adaptation (n.) 适应；改编
- adaptable (adj.) 可适应的
- adapter (n.) 适配器
