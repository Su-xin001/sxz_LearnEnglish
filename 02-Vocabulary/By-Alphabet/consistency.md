## 释义

**词性**：noun  
**英释**：the quality of always behaving or performing in a similar way  
**中释**：一致性，连贯性  

## 发音

<https://dict.youdao.com/dictvoice?audio=consistency&type=2>

## 例句

1. Consistency is the key to success in language learning.
2. There was no consistency in the company's marketing strategy.
3. She showed remarkable consistency in her performance throughout the year.

## 搭配

- maintain consistency — 保持一致性
- lack of consistency — 缺乏连贯性
- internal consistency — 内在一致性

## 同义词 / 反义词

- 同义词：steadiness, uniformity, reliability
- 反义词：inconsistency, variability

## 记忆方法

con(共同) + sist(站) + ency → 站在一起 → 一致性
