## 释义

**词性**：noun  
**英释**：deep mental involvement; the act of putting completely under liquid  
**中释**：沉浸，浸入  

## 发音

<https://dict.youdao.com/dictvoice?audio=immersion&type=2>

## 例句

1. Language immersion is one of the most effective learning methods.
2. The immersion in local culture transformed her understanding.
3. Total immersion in the subject helped him master it quickly.

## 搭配

- language immersion — 语言沉浸
- total immersion — 完全沉浸
- cultural immersion — 文化沉浸

## 同义词 / 反义词

- 同义词：absorption, engagement, submersion
- 反义词：detachment, withdrawal

## 记忆方法

im(进入) + mers(沉) + ion → 沉进去 → 沉浸
