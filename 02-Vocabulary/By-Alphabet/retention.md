## 释义

**词性**：noun  
**英释**：the ability to keep or continue to have something  
**中释**：记忆力，保持，保留  

## 发音

<https://dict.youdao.com/dictvoice?audio=retention&type=2>

## 例句

1. Spaced repetition improves long-term retention of vocabulary.
2. Employee retention is a major concern for many companies.
3. Water retention can cause swelling in the legs.

## 搭配

- knowledge retention — 知识保持
- memory retention — 记忆保持
- customer retention — 客户保留

## 同义词 / 反义词

- 同义词：preservation, maintenance, memory
- 反义词：loss, forgetting
