# abandon

## Definition

**v.** to leave completely and forever; to give up
**n.** a feeling of being free from restrictions and worries

## Examples

- He had to **abandon** his car in the snow.
- They **abandoned** the sinking ship.
- She danced with wild **abandon**.

## Collocations

- abandon hope 放弃希望
- abandon ship 弃船
- abandon a project 放弃项目
- with reckless abandon 不顾一切地

## Related Words

- abandonment (n.) 放弃
- abandoned (adj.) 被遗弃的
