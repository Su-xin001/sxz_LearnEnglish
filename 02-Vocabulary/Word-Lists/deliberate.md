## deliberate

### 释义
**音标**: /dɪˈlɪbərət/
**词性**: adj. / v.
**英文释义**: done on purpose; to think carefully about something
**中文释义**: 故意的；深思熟虑的；仔细考虑

### 例句
> She made a deliberate effort to improve her English pronunciation.
> 她刻意努力改善自己的英语发音。

### 词根词缀
de-(完全) + liber(称量/衡量) + -ate → 仔细衡量 → 深思熟虑的
同根词: liberty, liberal, liberate

### 同义词 / 反义词
- 同义词: intentional, purposeful, calculated
- 反义词: accidental, unintentional
