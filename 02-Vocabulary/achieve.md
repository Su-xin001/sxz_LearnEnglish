# achieve

## Definition

**v.** to successfully bring about or reach (a desired objective, level, or result)

## Examples

- She **achieved** her goal of running a marathon.
- What do you hope to **achieve** by doing this?
- They have **achieved** remarkable success in a short time.

## Collocations

- achieve success 取得成功
- achieve one's goal 达到目标
- achieve a balance 达到平衡
- achieve results 取得成果

## Related Words

- achievement (n.) 成就
- achievable (adj.) 可实现的
- overachieve (v.) 超额完成
