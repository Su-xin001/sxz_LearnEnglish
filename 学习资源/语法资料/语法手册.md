# 📘 英语核心语法手册

> 英语语法核心知识点速查

---

## 一、时态体系

### 1. 一般现在时

| 用法 | 示例 |
|------|------|
| 习惯性动作 | I study English every day. |
| 客观事实 | The earth revolves around the sun. |
| 计划安排 | The train leaves at 8 am tomorrow. |

### 2. 一般过去时

| 用法 | 示例 |
|------|------|
| 过去的动作 | I visited London last year. |
| 过去的习惯 | I used to play tennis. |

### 3. 现在完成时

| 用法 | 示例 |
|------|------|
| 过去影响现在 | I have lost my key. (still lost) |
| 经历 | I have been to Japan twice. |
| 持续到现在 | I have lived here for 10 years. |

### 4. 过去完成时

| 用法 | 示例 |
|------|------|
| 过去的过去 | I had finished dinner before she arrived. |

### 5. 进行时态

| 时态 | 示例 |
|------|------|
| 现在进行 | I am studying English now. |
| 过去进行 | I was reading when he called. |
| 现在完成进行 | I have been waiting for two hours. |

## 二、从句

### 1. 定语从句

| 类型 | 关系词 | 示例 |
|------|--------|------|
| 限制性 | who/which/that | The man **who** called you is my teacher. |
| 非限制性 | who/which | My father, **who** is 60, loves hiking. |
| 介词+关系词 | prep+which/whom | The house **in which** I live is old. |

### 2. 名词性从句

| 类型 | 连接词 | 示例 |
|------|--------|------|
| 主语从句 | that/whether | **That** he passed the exam surprised everyone. |
| 宾语从句 | that/if/whether | I know **that** she is honest. |
| 表语从句 | that/because | The reason is **that** I was ill. |
| 同位语从句 | that | The fact **that** the earth is round is known to all. |

### 3. 状语从句

| 类型 | 连接词 | 示例 |
|------|--------|------|
| 时间 | when/while/as | **While** I was reading, he called. |
| 条件 | if/unless/provided | **If** it rains, I will stay home. |
| 让步 | although/though/even if | **Although** he is young, he is wise. |
| 原因 | because/since/as | **Since** you insist, I will go. |
| 目的 | so that/in order that | Speak clearly **so that** everyone can hear. |

---

## 三、虚拟语气

| 类型 | 结构 | 示例 |
|------|------|------|
| 与现在相反 | If + did/were, would + do | If I **were** you, I **would** accept it. |
| 与过去相反 | If + had done, would have done | If I **had known**, I **would have** helped. |
| 与将来相反 | If + were to/should, would do | If it **should** rain, I **would** stay. |
| wish | wish + did/had done/would do | I wish I **were** taller. |
| suggest | suggest + (should) do | I suggest that he **(should) go** now. |

---

## 四、非谓语动词

### 1. 不定式 (to do)

| 用法 | 示例 |
|------|------|
| 目的 | I came **to help** you. |
| 宾语 | I want **to learn** English. |
| 定语 | I have something **to tell** you. |
| 补语 | He asked me **to wait**. |

### 2. 动名词 (doing)

| 用法 | 示例 |
|------|------|
| 主语 | **Swimming** is good exercise. |
| 宾语 | I enjoy **reading** books. |
| 介词宾语 | She is good at **writing**. |

### 3. 分词 (doing/done)

| 类型 | 示例 |
|------|------|
| 现在分词（主动/进行） | The **crying** baby needs milk. |
| 过去分词（被动/完成） | The **broken** window needs repair. |

---

## 五、特殊句式

### 1. 倒装句

| 类型 | 示例 |
|------|------|
| 完全倒装 | **On the table** lies a book. |
| 部分倒装 | **Never have** I seen such beauty. |
| Only前置 | **Only then did** I understand. |
| Not only前置 | **Not only did** he come, but he also helped. |

### 2. 强调句

| 结构 | 示例 |
|------|------|
| It is...that... | It **was** John **that** broke the window. |
| Do/Does/Did + 动词 | I **do** love you. |

### 3. 省略句

| 类型 | 示例 |
|------|------|
| 不定式省略 | You can go if you want **(to)**. |
| 状语从句省略 | **When (you are) in Rome**, do as Romans do. |
