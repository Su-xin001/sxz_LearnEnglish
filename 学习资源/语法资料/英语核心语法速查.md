# 英语核心语法速查

## 一、时态体系

| 时态 | 结构 | 用法 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|------|
| 一般现在时 | V/V-s | 习惯、事实 | She reads every day. |
| 一般过去时 | V-ed | 过去动作 | He visited Paris last year. |
| 一般将来时 | will + V | 将来计划 | I will call you tomorrow. |
| 现在进行时 | am/is/are + V-ing | 正在进行 | She is reading now. |
| 过去进行时 | was/were + V-ing | 过去某时正在 | I was sleeping at 10pm. |
| 现在完成时 | have/has + V-ed | 已完成影响现在 | I have finished the work. |
| 过去完成时 | had + V-ed | 过去之前完成 | She had left before I arrived. |
| 现在完成进行时 | have/has been + V-ing | 从过去持续到现在 | I have been studying for 3 hours. |

## 二、从句体系

### 定语从句

- 限制性：The book **that** I bought is interesting.
- 非限制性：Paris, **which** is the capital of France, is beautiful.

### 名词性从句

- 主语从句：**What** he said surprised everyone.
- 宾语从句：I know **that** she is right.
- 表语从句：The question is **whether** we should go.

### 状语从句

- 时间：**When** I arrived, she had left.
- 条件：**If** it rains, we will stay home.
- 让步：**Although** he was tired, he continued working.

## 三、虚拟语气

| 类型 | 结构 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| 与现在事实相反 | If + 过去式, would + V | If I were you, I would study harder. |
| 与过去事实相反 | If + had + V-ed, would have + V-ed | If I had known, I would have helped. |
| 与将来事实相反 | If + were to/should + V, would + V | If it should rain, we would cancel. |
| wish句型 | wish + 过去式/过去完成式 | I wish I were taller. |

## 四、非谓语动词

### 不定式 (to + V)

- 目的：I came **to help** you.
- 计划：She wants **to learn** English.

### 动名词 (V-ing)

- 享受：I enjoy **reading** books.
- 习惯：She is used to **getting** up early.

### 分词

- 现在分词（主动）：The **crying** baby needs milk.
- 过去分词（被动）：The **broken** window was repaired.

## 五、特殊句式

### 倒装句

- Never **have I** seen such beauty.
- Not only **did he** finish, but he also won.

### 强调句

- It **was** yesterday **that** I met her.
- I **do** believe you.

### 省略句

- If possible, (you should) come early.
- When (you are) in Rome, do as Romans do.
