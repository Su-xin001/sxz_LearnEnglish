# The Power of Reading

Reading is one of the most effective ways to improve your English skills. When you read regularly, you encounter new vocabulary in context, which helps you understand and remember words more effectively than simply memorizing lists.

## Why Reading Matters

Research has shown that extensive reading leads to significant improvements in vocabulary acquisition, reading fluency, and overall language proficiency. Unlike intensive reading, where you focus on every unknown word, extensive reading encourages you to read for pleasure and general understanding.

## Tips for Effective Reading

1. **Choose the right level** - Select materials that are slightly above your current level. You should understand about 90% of the words.
2. **Read regularly** - Even 15-20 minutes a day can make a big difference over time.
3. **Don't look up every word** - Try to guess the meaning from context first. Only look up words that appear frequently or are essential for understanding.
4. **Keep a vocabulary journal** - Write down interesting new words with their context and review them periodically.
5. **Re-read texts** - Reading the same text multiple times helps reinforce vocabulary and improves comprehension.
