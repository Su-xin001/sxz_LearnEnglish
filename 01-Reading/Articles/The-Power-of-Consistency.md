## 原文

Language learning is a journey that requires **patience** and ==consistency==. Unlike cramming for an exam, acquiring a new language demands regular practice over an extended period.

Research shows that ==spaced repetition== is one of the most effective techniques for vocabulary retention. By reviewing words at gradually increasing intervals, learners can move information from short-term to long-term memory.

Another crucial factor is ==immersion==. Surrounding yourself with the target language — through reading, listening, and speaking — creates neural pathways that make recall more natural and automatic.

The key takeaway is simple: ==consistency beats intensity==. Studying for 30 minutes every day is far more effective than studying for 3 hours once a week.

## 中文翻译

语言学习是一段需要耐心和坚持的旅程。与考前突击不同，掌握一门新语言需要长期的规律练习。

研究表明，间隔重复是最有效的词汇记忆技巧之一。通过逐渐增加间隔时间来复习单词，学习者可以将信息从短期记忆转移到长期记忆中。

另一个关键因素是沉浸。让自己置身于目标语言环境中——通过阅读、听力和口语——可以创建神经通路，使回忆更加自然和自动。

核心要点很简单：坚持胜过强度。每天学习30分钟远比一周一次学习3小时更有效。

## 阅读笔记

这篇文章强调了语言学习中最重要的三个原则：
1. **坚持**：每天少量学习比偶尔大量学习更有效
2. **间隔重复**：科学复习方法能显著提高记忆效率
3. **沉浸式学习**：多维度接触目标语言
