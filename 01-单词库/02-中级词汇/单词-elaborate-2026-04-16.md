# 📖 elaborate

## 音标
- 🇬🇧 UK: /ɪˈlæbərət/
- 🇺🇸 US: /ɪˈlæbərət/

## 词性与释义

### adj.

1. **常用释义**：精心制作的；详尽的；复杂的
2. **僻义**：煞费苦心的

### v.

1. **常用释义**：详细阐述；详细说明
2. **僻义**：精心制作

## 例句

1. She elaborated on her ideas during the meeting.
   - 🇬🇧 She elaborated on her ideas during the meeting.
   - 🇨🇳 她在会议上详细阐述了她的想法。

2. The elaborate design of the palace took years to complete.
   - 🇬🇧 The elaborate design of the palace took years to complete.
   - 🇨🇳 宫殿精心的设计花了数年才完成。

## 记忆方法

### 词根词缀分析
- **e-** (出) + **labor** (劳动) + **-ate** (形容词/动词后缀)
- 字面意思：付出大量劳动做出来的 → 精心制作的

### 联想记忆
- elaborate → e(出) + labor(劳动) + ate(吃) → 为了吃顿好的，出了很多劳动 → 精心制作的

## 搭配与用法

| 搭配 | 含义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| elaborate on | 详细阐述 | Could you elaborate on that point? |
| elaborate design | 精心设计 | The elaborate design impressed everyone. |
| elaborate plan | 详尽的计划 | They made an elaborate plan for the trip. |

## 相关词汇

- 同义词：detailed, intricate, complex, sophisticated
- 反义词：simple, plain, basic
- 派生词：elaboration (n.), elaborately (adv.)
