# 📖 resilient

## 音标
- 🇬🇧 UK: /rɪˈzɪliənt/
- 🇺🇸 US: /rɪˈzɪliənt/

## 词性与释义

### adj.

1. **常用释义**：有弹性的；能迅速恢复的；适应力强的
2. **僻义**：弹回的；跃回的

## 例句

1. Children are often more resilient than adults think.
   - 🇬🇧 Children are often more resilient than adults think.
   - 🇨🇳 孩子们通常比大人认为的更有恢复力。

2. The resilient economy bounced back quickly after the crisis.
   - 🇬🇧 The resilient economy bounced back quickly after the crisis.
   - 🇨🇳 有弹性的经济在危机后迅速反弹。

## 记忆方法

### 词根词缀分析
- **resili-** (弹回) + **-ent** (形容词后缀，表示"具有...性质的")
- 来自拉丁语 resilire "跳回"

### 联想记忆
- resilient → re(回) + sili(跳) + ent → 能跳回来的 → 有弹性的/能恢复的
- 联想弹簧：压下去还能弹回来 → resilient

## 搭配与用法

| 搭配 | 含义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| resilient economy | 有弹性的经济 | The country has a resilient economy. |
| resilient children | 适应力强的孩子 | Resilient children cope better with stress. |
| highly resilient | 高度弹性的 | The material is highly resilient. |

## 相关词汇

- 同义词：tough, adaptable, flexible, robust
- 反义词：fragile, vulnerable, delicate
- 派生词：resilience (n.), resiliently (adv.)
