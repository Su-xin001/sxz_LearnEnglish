# 📖 ubiquitous

## 音标
- 🇬🇧 UK: /juːˈbɪkwɪtəs/
- 🇺🇸 US: /juːˈbɪkwɪtəs/

## 词性与释义

### adj.

1. **常用释义**：无处不在的；普遍存在的
2. **僻义**：似乎无处不在的

## 例句

1. Smartphones have become ubiquitous in modern life.
   - 🇬🇧 Smartphones have become ubiquitous in modern life.
   - 🇨🇳 智能手机在现代生活中无处不在。

2. The ubiquitous presence of social media affects everyone's daily routine.
   - 🇬🇧 The ubiquitous presence of social media affects everyone's daily routine.
   - 🇨🇳 社交媒体无处不在的影响着每个人的日常生活。

## 记忆方法

### 词根词缀分析
- **ubique** (拉丁语，到处) + **-ous** (形容词后缀，表示"充满...的")
- 来自拉丁语 ubique "到处，各处"

### 联想记忆
- ubiquitous → ubi(哪里) + quit(离开) + ous → 哪里都离不开的 → 无处不在的
- 联想：U-BIKE(共享单车) → 到处都有 → ubiquitous

## 搭配与用法

| 搭配 | 含义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| ubiquitous presence | 无处不在的存在 | The ubiquitous presence of WiFi... |
| become ubiquitous | 变得无处不在 | Plastic has become ubiquitous. |
| ubiquitous computing | 泛在计算 | Ubiquitous computing is the future. |

## 相关词汇

- 同义词：omnipresent, pervasive, widespread, universal
- 反义词：rare, scarce, uncommon
- 派生词：ubiquity (n.), ubiquitously (adv.)
