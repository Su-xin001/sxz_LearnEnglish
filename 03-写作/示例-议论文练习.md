# 示例-议论文练习

## 题目

Some people think that AI tools help students learn English more efficiently. Do you agree or disagree?

## 正文

I agree that AI tools help students learn English more efficiently. First, AI can provide instant feedback on grammar and pronunciation. Second, AI-powered apps make it convenient to practice anytime. However, students should not rely on AI completely because real communication still needs human interaction.
