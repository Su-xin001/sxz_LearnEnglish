### 语法要点
1. **现在进行时的被动语态**
   - 结构：am/is/are + being + 过去分词
   - 例句：More trees are being planted to combat climate change.

2. **定语从句**
   - 结构：先行词 + 关系代词 + 从句
   - 例句：The gases that trap heat are called greenhouse gases.

3. **条件句**
   - 结构：If + 一般现在时，will + 动词原形
   - 例句：If we continue to burn fossil fuels, global warming will worsen.
