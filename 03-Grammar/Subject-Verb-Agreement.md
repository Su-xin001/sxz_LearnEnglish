## Subject-Verb Agreement (主谓一致)

### 语法规则

主谓一致是指句子中的谓语动词必须与主语在人称和数上保持一致。

**基本规则：**
1. 单数主语 + 单数动词: *The cat **runs** fast.*
2. 复数主语 + 复数动词: *The cats **run** fast.*
3. 不可数名词视为单数: *The water **is** cold.*

**特殊情况：**
- **Each / Every + 单数名词** → 单数动词
  - Each student **has** a book.
- **Neither...nor / Either...or** → 就近原则
  - Neither the teacher nor the students **were** present.
- **The number of + 复数名词** → 单数动词
  - The number of students **is** increasing.
- **A number of + 复数名词** → 复数动词
  - A number of students **are** waiting.

### 例句

| 主语 | 动词 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| 单数 | is/has/runs | The information **is** reliable. |
| 复数 | are/have/run | The data **are** conflicting. |
| 集合名词 | 视语境 | The team **is** winning. (整体) |
| 集合名词 | 视语境 | The team **are** arguing. (个体) |

### 常见错误

❌ *The list of items are on the desk.* (主语是 list，不是 items)
✅ *The list of items **is** on the desk.*

❌ *Everyone have their own opinion.*
✅ *Everyone **has** their own opinion.*

### 练习

1. The group of students _____ (is/are) going to the museum.
2. Neither the cat nor the dogs _____ (was/were) outside.
3. The news _____ (is/are) very surprising.
