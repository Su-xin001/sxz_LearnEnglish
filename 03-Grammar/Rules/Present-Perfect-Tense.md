## 规则说明

现在完成时（Present Perfect Tense）表示过去发生的动作对现在造成的影响或结果，或者表示从过去开始持续到现在的动作或状态。

## 结构公式

**肯定句**：主语 + have/has + 过去分词  
**否定句**：主语 + have/has + not + 过去分词  
**疑问句**：Have/Has + 主语 + 过去分词?

## 例句

### 肯定句

1. I **have studied** English for three years.
2. She **has visited** London twice.
3. They **have finished** their homework.

### 否定句

1. I **haven't seen** that movie yet.
2. He **hasn't completed** the assignment.
3. We **haven't decided** on a plan.

### 疑问句

1. **Have** you **ever been** to Japan?
2. **Has** she **arrived** yet?
3. **Have** they **started** the meeting?

## 常见错误

1. ❌ I have went to Beijing. → ✅ I have **been** to Beijing.
2. ❌ She has saw this film. → ✅ She has **seen** this film.
3. ❌ I have lived here since 3 years. → ✅ I have lived here **for** 3 years.

## 练习

1. I _____ (finish) my homework already.
2. She _____ (not/visit) the museum yet.
3. _____ you ever _____ (eat) sushi?

## 单词卡片

现在完成时::have/has + 过去分词，表示过去动作对现在的影响
