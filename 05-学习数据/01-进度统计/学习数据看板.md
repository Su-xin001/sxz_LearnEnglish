### 本月学习趋势

```dataview
TABLE
  date as 日期,
  length(filter(file.outlinks, (l) => contains(string(l), "02-单词库"))) as 新学单词,
  reading_time as 阅读时长
FROM "03-学习笔记/01-每日笔记"
WHERE date >= date(today) - dur(30 days)
SORT date DESC
```
